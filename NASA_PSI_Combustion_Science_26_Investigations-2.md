### 01. ACME — Burning Rate Emulator (BRE)

- **PSI number:** **PSI-20**
- **Areas:**
  - **Research area:** Combustion Science
  - **Subresearch area (PSI index):** Flames of Gaseous Fuels
  - **Scientific focus:** Gaseous combustion; flame burning rates and spacecraft material flammability
- **Types:**
  - **Project setting / type:** ISS microgravity experiment
  - **PSI record type:** Investigation
- **DOI:** [10.60555/vb93-3y72](https://doi.org/10.60555/vb93-3y72)

#### Objectives

Determine how ventilation, oxygen availability, and fuel supply affect burning rates in low-gravity environments, including near-extinction behavior relevant to fire safety.

#### **Approach**

Use an ACME modular burner in the ISS Combustion Integrated Rack to establish controlled, low-buoyancy flames. Change the gas flow and oxidizer conditions and observe flame stability and fuel consumption.

**Sources:** [NASA PSI investigation record](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-20) · [PSI accession/DOI archive](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Krypton-N/NASA-SPACE-APPS/main/data/psi/psi_index.csv) · [NASA background](https://www.nasa.gov/physical-sciences-informatics-psi/psi-investigations-by-research-area/) · [NASA background](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/space-stations-quest-for-the-secrets-of-fire/)

### 02. ACME — Coflow Laminar Diffusion Flame (CLD Flame)

- **PSI number:** **PSI-21**
- **Areas:**
  - **Research area:** Combustion Science
  - **Subresearch area (PSI index):** Flames of gaseous fuel
  - **Scientific focus:** Gas-phase diffusion flames; soot formation and oxidation
- **Types:**
  - **Project setting / type:** ISS microgravity experiment
  - **PSI record type:** Investigation
- **DOI:** [10.60555/23e1-9950](https://doi.org/10.60555/23e1-9950)

#### Objectives

Acquire benchmark measurements of nonpremixed diffusion flames across sooty and highly diluted conditions and improve models of soot production and flame structure.

#### **Approach**

Burn gaseous fuel in a coflow burner under carefully controlled fuel and oxidizer flows. Record flame shape, luminosity, and soot-related behavior under microgravity so predictions can be compared with measurements.

**Sources:** [NASA PSI investigation record](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-21) · [PSI accession/DOI archive](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Krypton-N/NASA-SPACE-APPS/main/data/psi/psi_index.csv) · [NASA background](https://science.nasa.gov/physical-sciences-informatics-psi/nasa-psi-research-opportunity/) · [NASA background](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/space-stations-quest-for-the-secrets-of-fire/)

### 03. ACME — Cool Flames Investigation with Gases (CFI-G)

- **PSI number:** **PSI-159**
- **Areas:**
  - **Research area:** Combustion Science
  - **Subresearch area (PSI index):** Flames of Gaseous Fuels
  - **Scientific focus:** Low-temperature oxidation; gaseous cool flames
- **Types:**
  - **Project setting / type:** ISS microgravity experiment
  - **PSI record type:** Investigation
- **DOI:** [10.60555/cyt1-kh64](https://doi.org/10.60555/cyt1-kh64)

### 08. Burning and Suppression of Solids — II (BASS-II)

- **PSI number:** **PSI-25**
- **Areas:**
  - **Research area:** Combustion Science
  - **Subresearch area (PSI index):** Spacecraft Fire Safety
  - **Scientific focus:** Spacecraft Fire Safety
- **Types:**
  - **Project setting / type:** Microgravity Investigation; ISS
  - **PSI record type:** Investigation
- **DOI:** [10.60555/4qc4-de67](https://doi.org/10.60555/4qc4-de67)

#### Objectives

Characterize ignition, flame growth, spread, and extinction limits of solid fuels in weak forced flow; compare these results with normal-gravity flammability screening and provide data for future fire-safety experiments.

#### **Approach**

Carry out crew-assisted burning tests in a small wind tunnel installed inside the ISS Microgravity Science Glovebox. Mount rods, sheets, films, and other geometries; vary airflow up to approximately 40 cm/s and reduce oxygen concentration by nitrogen dilution. Image and assess flame appearance and spread in concurrent and opposed flow.

### 09. Computational and Experimental Analysis of SPICE Data Sets

- **PSI number:** **PSI-60**
- **Areas:**
  - **Research area:** Combustion Science
  - **Subresearch area (PSI index):** Gaseous
  - **Scientific focus:** Gaseous combustion; flame pyrometry and soot-formation models
- **Types:**
  - **Project setting / type:** Ground Investigation; PSI data reanalysis
  - **PSI record type:** Investigation
- **DOI:** [10.60555/mzjm-yv57](https://doi.org/10.60555/mzjm-yv57)

#### Objectives

Quantify soot temperatures, soot volume fractions, and the impact of radiation and reaction-model assumptions on computed coflow diffusion flames.

#### **Approach**

Decode Nikon NEF images from SPICE into color channels, use color-ratio pyrometry and calibrated luminosity to derive soot temperature and concentration fields. Implement coupled flow, species, and soot simulations including radiation reabsorption and sectional aggregate formation; compare Yale and ACME burner cases in normal and reduced gravity.

### 10. Concurrent Flame Spread Modeling using Flamelet Generated Manifolds in Microgravity with Comparison to BASS Experiments using Two-Color Tomography

- **PSI number:** **PSI-62**
- **Areas:**
  - **Research area:** Combustion Science
  - **Subresearch area (PSI index):** Spacecraft Fire Safety
  - **Scientific focus:** Spacecraft Fire Safety
- **Types:**
  - **Project setting / type:** Ground Investigation
  - **PSI record type:** Investigation
- **DOI:** [10.60555/e9xp-ex83](https://doi.org/10.60555/e9xp-ex83)

#### Objectives

Develop and validate unsteady flamelet generated manifold (UFGM) models for concurrent flame spread in microgravity, using BASS data to improve predictions of material flammability limits.

  - **Subresearch area (PSI index):** Spacecraft Fire Safety

- **Types:**
  - **Project setting / type:** Microgravity Investigation; ISS

- **DOI:** [10.60555/ztgj-d202](https://doi.org/10.60555/ztgj-d202)

### 13. Effect of External Thermo-Convective Perturbation on Cool Flame Dynamics: A Multidimensional Multi-Physics CFD Analysis

- **PSI number:** **PSI-117**
- **Areas:**
  - **Research area:** Combustion Science
  - **Subresearch area (PSI index):** Droplet
  - **Scientific focus:** Droplet cool flames; computational fluid dynamics and thermal perturbations
- **Types:**
  - **Project setting / type:** Ground Investigation; numerical modeling
  - **PSI record type:** Investigation
- **DOI:** [10.60555/gz0q-b577](https://doi.org/10.60555/gz0q-b577)

#### Objectives

Determine how weak convection, external heating, and their interaction influence initiation, oscillation, and extinction of microgravity droplet cool flames.

#### **Approach**

Develop a three-dimensional OpenFOAM CFD model of n-alkane droplets with fluid dynamics, low-temperature chemistry, transport, and radiation. Validate with ISS FLEX data, then test droplet drift, imposed heat flux, and combined forcing over multiple droplet sizes and ambient conditions to map cool-flame stability.

### 16. Quantitative Studies of Cool Flame Transitions at Radiation/Stretch Extinction Using Counterflow Flames

- **PSI number:** **PSI-142**
- **Areas:**
  - **Research area:** Combustion Science
  - **Subresearch area (PSI index):** Spacecraft Fire Safety
  - **Scientific focus:** Low-temperature flame transition; extinction and oxygen effects
- **Types:**
  - **Project setting / type:** Ground Investigation; modeling and controlled flame studies
  - **PSI record type:** Investigation
- **DOI:** [10.60555/80qf-vj95](https://doi.org/10.60555/80qf-vj95)

### 19. Smoke Point in Co-flow Experiment (SPICE)

- **PSI number:** **PSI-107**
- **Areas:**
  - **Research area:** Combustion Science
  - **Subresearch area (PSI index):** Fire Safety
  - **Scientific focus:** Fire Safety
- **Types:**
  - **Project setting / type:** Microgravity Investigation; ISS
  - **PSI record type:** Investigation
- **DOI:** [10.60555/9kbh-e962](https://doi.org/10.60555/9kbh-e962)

#### Objectives

Identify smoke-point properties of nonbuoyant laminar jet diffusion flames, including the effect of burner diameter and coflow velocity on luminous flame length and smoke onset.

### 26. Utilization of SAME Data for Advanced Modeling and Simulation of Smoke Generation in Micro-Gravity

- **PSI number:** **PSI-115**
- **Areas:**
  - **Research area:** Combustion Science
  - **Subresearch area (PSI index):** Smoke Aerosol, Combustion Simulation
  - **Scientific focus:** Smoke aerosol dynamics and transport; numerical simulations
- **Types:**
  - **Project setting / type:** Ground Investigation; archived data reanalysis
  - **PSI record type:** Investigation
